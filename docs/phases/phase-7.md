# Phase 7 — Business Alignment

## Goal

Ensure the running product, the pitch, and the Lean Business Canvas tell one coherent story: who the users are, what pain is solved, how the product works, how it makes money/sustains itself, and what impact it can measure.

## What Is Already Present (from Phase 0)

- `planning.md` with problem statement, target users, solution, implemented scope, and decisions.
- `README.md` with user-facing description, features, and quick start.
- A working product that demonstrates the core value proposition (early water/crop stress warnings for South African farmers).

## Scope IN

1. Finalise the Lean Business Canvas.
2. Align the product UI/demo with the Canvas story (target user, value prop, channels, metrics).
3. Prepare the final slide deck and 90-second pitch.
4. Define and surface impact metrics in the app/demo.
5. Submit final hackathon files (SSDLC, deck, demo video).

## Scope OUT

- New product engineering.
- Code changes not required to support the pitch narrative.
- Live commercial partnerships or real user acquisition.

## Definition of Done

1. `/docs/LEAN_BUSINESS_CANVAS.md` exists and every block is supported by the running product or a credible near-term plan.
2. `/docs/IMPACT_METRICS.md` exists with at least 3 quantitative metrics the demo can reference.
3. The final slide deck is complete, ≤ 10 slides, and includes a demo screenshot/clip on every product claim.
4. The 90-second pitch narration matches the demo video timing.
5. The SSDLC submission is complete and reviewed.
6. All hackathon submission files are uploaded before the deadline.
7. No P0/P1 technical-debt items remain open.

## Deliverables

- `/docs/IMPACT_METRICS.md`.
- Final slide deck (linked or in repo).

## Risks / Notes

- The 20-response threshold for validation stats means the agreement-rate metric cannot be demoed as "validated". Use the provisional badge and explain the data-collection plan.
- Keep the pitch grounded in the Eastern Cape demo region; avoid overclaiming national scale without evidence.
